About Coastal Guides

Coastal Guides is a field-note archive for the shoreline between Harborview and the old lighthouse — tide charts, harbor walks, and the odd cottage review, written by whoever's boots are still wet.

Maren keeps the rental listings honest; the rest of us just keep showing up when the fog rolls in.
